# M1 batch 1 — proposed pre-AO definitions

Status: **proposed, not integrated and not verified**. These are original PT-PT
definitions offered for review; they are not quotations and not independent
lexicographic certification. Until they are approved, the lemmas stay in
`docs/missing-lemmas.csv` and in the M1 queue.

## Why these lemmas

At the time of writing, the 274 remaining M1 lemmas are all in the pre-AO-only
group: the modern lemma and its pre-AO spelling are both absent from Dicionário
Aberto, so no spelling mapping can resolve them. They are genuine definition
gaps.

This batch is a first selection of 35 lemmas chosen for likely frequency in
ordinary running text. It includes all eight M1 lemmas that appear in the
12,036-word coverage list (`docs/WORDLIST-COVERAGE.md`): `eletrónico`,
`eletrónica`, `detetar`, `deteção`, `direcional`, `bidirecional`, `autoestima`
and `atualizado`. That list holds unique words, so membership is evidence that
the word occurs in that sample, not a measure of frequency.

## Review questions

- Are the definitions for `anticoncecional`, `detetive` and `direcionar`
  acceptable as written, or do they need a second sense?
- Should `eletrónico` and `eletrónica` mention the Brazilian Portuguese usage?
- Should `para-brisas` note the Brazilian equivalent `para-brisa`?
- Is `telespetador` the preferred label, or should the entry keep both
  `telespetador` and `telespectador` as forms?
- The batch follows AO90 in joining `auto-`, `co-`, `contra-` and `anti-` to the
  base word when the elements are not independent. Confirm that the entries for
  `autoestrada`, `autoajuda`, `autorretrato` and `autossuficiência` should keep
  the joined form as the headword and the hyphenated form as the pre-AO alias.

## Remaining queue

The other 239 pre-AO-only lemmas stay in `docs/M1-MISSING.md`. They are
clustered around predictable families — `eletro-`, `co-`, `contra-`,
`para-`, `anti-`, `-oide` — and several are technical or rare, so they are
better handled in later batches with the same review step.

## How approval works

Approved lines move to `sources/curated-definitions.txt` in the
`lemma|part-of-speech|definition` format, then the corpus and runtime data are
rebuilt and the release identifier is bumped. A proposal is not evidence: do not
treat an integrated editorial definition as a source-backed or human-verified
fact.
