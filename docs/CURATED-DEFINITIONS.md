# Curated missing lemmas — approved editorial definitions

Status: **approved and integrated as a labelled editorial layer**. The
canonical structured source is `sources/curated-definitions.txt`; the app shows
the wording as an approved Lexi2 editorial definition, separately from the
Dicionário Aberto source. These are original definitions, not quotations and
not independent lexicographic certification.

The notes in `sources/curated-lemmas.txt` remain review hints, not definitions.
The two curated lemmas that already had an exact Dicionário Aberto entry
(`auscultador` and `servidor`) keep the source definition and are not overridden.
