canonical structured source is `sources/curated-definitions.txt`. These are
concise PT-PT definitions for the 19 M1 lemmas whose only automatic suggestion
was an unsafe old-spelling candidate. The flagged usage, pre-AO and regional
notes are kept inside the definitions shown by the app. They are original
editorial wording, not quotations or independent lexicographic certification.

The table below gives the core approved definition. Where the note column
records a usage, pre-AO or regional caveat, the app and the canonical source
append that caveat to the definition as a parenthetical sentence, so the text a
reader sees is slightly longer than the summary here.
